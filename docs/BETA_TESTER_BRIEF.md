# Beta tester brief — Web Swing Replay (pose-only)

**What this is:** a closed web beta. Upload a swing clip from your phone browser, wait a few minutes, review pose overlay + phase scores. Ball and racket tracking are **off**.

**URL:** the hosted Fly app the operator sent you. Sign in with the magic link emailed to you (expires in 15 minutes).

## Film this way

1. Side-on, landscape, full body in frame.
2. **20–30 seconds** of actual swings, good light.
3. Stay still. Skip walking-back-to-serve dead time.

See in-app **How to film** (`/how-to-film`) or open Upload with `?tour=1` for the coach marks.

## What you will see

- Label: **Pose + swing phases (beta)**.
- Empty ball/racket overlays is expected.
- A low-pose banner means re-film side-on in better light.

## Feedback

Use the floating **Feedback** button on Analysis and History (rating 1–5 + what felt wrong). Screenshots in the tester Slack/Telegram channel help.

## Privacy

Each tester only sees their own uploads. Delete a session from History to cascade-remove the video, pose files, jobs, and related notes. See `/privacy`.

## Out of scope this round

Ball overlay, TrackNet, racket-head speed, match rallies/heatmaps, YouTube ingest, TestFlight.
